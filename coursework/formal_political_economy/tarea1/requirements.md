## Minimum requirements:

* Formulation of a verifiable hypothesis about strategic behavior or coordination.
* Construction of a formal model that incorporates rational actors, incentives, rules, and strategic interactions.
* Derivation of a prediction or equilibrium from the stated assumptions.
* Conceptual framework and coherence with formal political economy approaches.

## Evaluation criteria:

| Criterion | Description | Weighting |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Theoretical consistency** | Clarity of assumptions, model structure, and its logical coherence | 30% |
| **Conceptual foundation** | Articulation with the reviewed literature and clarity of the theoretical framework | 30% |
| **Originality / relevance of the hypothesis** | Level of creativity or connection with real collective decision problems | 20% |
| **Presentation and argumentation** | Clarity, order, and formal rigor of the document | 20% |
